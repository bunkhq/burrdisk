# Burrdisk website (landing page)

Mode: Persuade. Static page in `site/`, published to GitHub Pages (bunkhq.github.io/burrdisk). Visitors: Mac and Windows owners who just hit a storage warning, plus developers who know caches pile up. Action: Download (OS auto-picked, other OS one click away) and View on GitHub, equal weight. Proof: the product's own drawings (burr, sunburst, Collector) animated on scroll, not playable; any disk data is a labelled example. No invented stats, users, testimonials or benchmarks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Open on the storage warning the visitor just got, then dig through it: the full bar bristles into a burr and the page descends disk → folder → Collector → Trash, one stage per section. Refuses the split hero with a screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md as shipped: graphite ground, Ember lit keys with halos, Fullness spines, the OKLCH data wheel, Trash Red only for the Trash stage, Mona Sans (display at 800 weight and 88% width: Black at 80% clogged at 88px with spines behind it, so the build backed off one step, with the user's 'Dig right in' tagline as the h1). No cards, no outlines. Raise (Darkroom Safelight Bay): stages in fixed order, never shuffled. Raise (Luminescent Understory): only the stage in view glows in data color; the rest sits in graphite.

STORY: "That warning is about my disk; this shows me where the space went, lets me pick, and only ever moves things to the Trash. It's free, on my OS."

FIRST VIEWPORT: Center, a Menu Ground alert: "Macintosh HD", "Your disk is almost full.", "432 GB used of 460 GB", a 94% bar ending in Fullness red. Behind it a ~700px unscanned burr whose lit spines grow out on load. Below: "Dig right in. Free up your disk." and the two equal actions. Nav: wordmark burr, How it works, Safety, GitHub.

# Home (disk picker)

Mode: Operate. Visitor job: pick a disk (or folder) and get into its scan in one click; manage cached scans (rescan, admin rescan, forget, reveal, Get Info) from a menu beside each disk's scan button.

## Direction contract

FORM: seed:1b97a9aa89c274ea (chose IMPECCABLE'S PICK, "Burr").

THESIS: Every disk is drawn as a burr, the prickly seed the product is named for: a dark seed core with radial spines. Refuses the category default of rounded cards with a ring gauge, a big percent and stat chips.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black loam ground, no card containers on home. Orange stays the brand accent; spines wear the sunburst's OKLCH data palette. System sans, tabular figures, one plain sentence of capacity instead of a stat block. Spines are drawn hairline-to-tapered paths, core is a filled disc with a thin orange rim when a scan is cached.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Titlebar (wordmark, Scan Folder…). Startup disk: large burr left, name + one capacity sentence + split Scan/Open button with menu right. Other drives: a row of small burrs, each with name, free space and its own split button. Disk images: compact list, each with a menu button.

SIGNATURE MOVE: Unscanned burr: spines around the full circle, the used fraction lit in fullness color, the rest dim stubs. Scanned burr: spines become the disk's real top-level folders, angle and length from their size, in sunburst colors, so home previews what is inside. Spines grow out on entrance and bristle (extend) on hover.

STORY: Name explains itself the first time a user sees it; scanned disks become recognisable at a glance.

RISK: literal reading of the name; keep the burr data-true, never ornamental.
